🎯 How to Use Pro Dance Studio

Pro Dance Studio draws a full-body cartoon dancer whose thumkas, steps and hair flips are locked to the music's beat grid. Choose from built-in copyright-free tracks or upload your own song, then watch the performance on a live disco stage — with reactive speakers, rotating floor spots and beat-synced particles.

  1. 🎵 Pick your audio. Choose a built-in genre (Pop, EDM, Hip-Hop, Slow), upload your own MP3/WAV up to 20MB, or select No Music to dance silently to a default beat.
  2. 🎛️ Shape the show (optional). Volume, beat sensitivity and dance energy; four outfit colors for the dancer.
  3. ▶ Press Play. The dancer runs its eight rotating choreography sets while BPM and move names update live.
  4. 🎬 Record or snapshot. HD/Standard sets the real encoding bitrate; recording captures the composited stage. Export the WebM or grab a PNG frame.
  5. 🔄 Reset clears music, recordings and counters.
  • 📌 Use 128 kbps MP3s for smaller files or 320 kbps for audiophile quality; the dancer syncs to either.
  • 📌 Beat Sensitivity adjusts how strongly the stage reacts — turn it down for softer songs, up for EDM.
  • 📌 Keep this tab visible while recording so frames and audio stay in sync. Spacebar toggles play/pause when the tool is in focus.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of music can I use?

You can choose from four built-in copyright-free genres (Pop, EDM, Hip-Hop, Slow) or upload your own MP3/WAV file up to 20MB. The tool detects tempo automatically.

Does the dancer react to the music?

Yes. The dancer's moves, hip sway, arm swings and hair physics are all locked to the beat grid. The speakers, lights and particles also react to bass in real time.

What format is the recorded video?

WebM with audio (VP9 or VP8 plus Opus, depending on browser support), which uploads directly to YouTube, WhatsApp and Instagram. HD or Standard sets the actual encoding bitrate before recording.

Are my files sent anywhere?

No. Songs and recordings never leave the device. Every file is checked by extension, MIME type and actual file signature before loading, and all object URLs and recording streams are released when you reset or close the page.

Does the tool work without any music?

Yes. Select No Music to dance silently to a default 120 BPM beat clock — the dancer still performs all eight choreography sets.

Everything runs locally in your browser, and a page refresh wipes all media from memory. No account, no uploads, no watermarks.
