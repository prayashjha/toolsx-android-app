✅ How to Use AI Dance Video Maker

Create a dancing video from your photo in a few simple steps:

  1. Choose music – select a built-in copyright-free track or upload your own MP3/WAV.
  2. Upload a photo – use a clear PNG/JPEG image (max 5MB).
  3. Select a theme – Auto, Bhajan, Romantic, Energetic, or Party.
  4. Press Play – the dancer will move with beat-sync and mouth-sync.
  5. Record video – click Record Video to capture the performance.
  6. Download – save the result as HD or SD WebM.

💡 Tip: For best results, use a photo with a clear face and good lighting.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free. No hidden charges, no premium version, no credit system. You can generate and download unlimited videos for personal or commercial use.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is used only in your browser's memory. No server upload, complete privacy.

What format is the downloaded video?

WebM format (VP9 codec + Opus audio). Easy to upload to YouTube, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook. HD and SD quality options available.

Can I get a copyright strike?

Built-in music tracks are procedurally synthesized — 100% copyright-free and royalty-free. When uploading your own song, ensure it's copyright-free or you have the rights.

Is mouth-sync real face tracking?

No. It's a lightweight stylised effect based on audio volume. Real face tracking would need heavy AI models. Our approach is 100% offline, battery-friendly, and privacy-safe.

Does recording work on mobile?

Yes — Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet (Android) and iOS 15+ Safari support MediaRecorder. Keep the screen on during recording.

What is the maximum video length?

Video length depends on your audio. Recording stops automatically when the song ends and the video downloads. Built-in tracks are 15-30 seconds.

Can I use multiple photos?

Currently one photo per video for single-dancer focus. For multiple photos, create separate videos and combine them in a video editor.
