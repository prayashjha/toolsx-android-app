🎬 How to Create Your Visual

  1. Add your track — drag & drop or click the upload box. Accepted: MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A (50 MB max).
  2. Pick a visual style — Bars, Wave, Ring, Radial, or Spectrum.
  3. Choose your palette — four presets, a custom canvas colour, or hit Random.
  4. Preview — hit Play, tweak the volume, and toggle Loop if you want it to repeat.
  5. Capture — press Record Full Video to render the whole song. A WebM file downloads when it finishes.
  6. Or save a still — click Save Frame as PNG to grab a single frame.

Pro tip: Chrome or Edge give the smoothest recording. Mobile browsers work too.

❓ Common Questions

Do I have to pay or sign up?

Nope. Everything is free, no account required, and there are no watermarks on the output.

Which audio formats work?

MP3, WAV, OGG and M4A up to 50 MB. Decoding is done by your browser's own audio engine.

What format is the recorded video?

WebM (VP9 or VP8 video + Opus audio). It plays back on YouTube and most modern players, and can be converted to MP4 with any editor.

Can I customise the look?

Yes — four ready-made palettes, a full colour picker for the canvas background, and a randomiser button for instant inspiration.

Is fullscreen supported?

Absolutely. Tap the Full button to expand the canvas across the screen.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Never. Decoding, drawing and recording all happen locally via the Web Audio and Canvas APIs. Your audio never touches a server.
