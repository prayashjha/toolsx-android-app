🎯 Tools-X Photo Studio Pro — Advanced Browser-Based Photo Editor

Tools-X Photo Studio Pro is a professional-grade online photo editor with crop, resize, filters, adjustments, undo/redo, and keyboard shortcuts. Everything runs directly in your browser.

✨ Key Features

  • 12 Filter Presets: Vivid, Noir, Vintage, Retro, Warm, Cool, Drama, Fade, Dreamy, Pop and more
  • 8 Adjustment Sliders: Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Blur, Grayscale, Sepia, Invert, Hue Rotate
  • Undo/Redo: 20-step history with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y keyboard shortcuts
  • Transform Tools: Rotate 90° left/right, Flip Horizontal/Vertical
  • Multi-Format Export: PNG (lossless), JPEG (compressed), WEBP (modern)
  • Privacy-First: All processing in your browser — no data leaves your device

🔒 100% Secure & Private

Images are never uploaded anywhere. All editing happens locally on your device. Your photos, confidential documents, and sensitive images remain completely safe.

💡 Pro Tips

  • JPEG Export: Keep quality at 90%+ for best results
  • WEBP Format: Use for web — smaller size, same quality
  • PNG Format: Supports transparency — best for logos
  • Undo/Redo: Use Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y for quick corrections

🎓 How to Use — Quick Guide

  1. Load Image: Click "Open" or drag and drop your file
  2. Adjust: Move sliders for brightness, contrast, saturation
  3. Filter: Select from 12 presets
  4. Transform: Rotate or flip as needed
  5. Export: Choose format and quality, click "Export"
