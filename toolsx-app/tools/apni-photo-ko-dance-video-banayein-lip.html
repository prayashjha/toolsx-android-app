Automated Audio Beat-Synced Dance Generation

Creating animated motion synchronized with music traditionally requires keyframing each hit and downbeat manually. This client-side choreography engine decodes live audio buffers directly inside your web browser, dynamically driving joint transforms, bounce velocity, and step routines in real time without external processing.

  1. Upload Any Music File: Click the drop area to select an MP3 or WAV track from your phone or computer. The dance animation triggers immediately once the audio buffer is loaded.

How Client-Side Beat Tracking Works

When an audio track is chosen, the browser's AudioContext interface decodes the raw binary data into an uncompressed PCM stream. A Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) node breaks this sound wave into frequency bins at 60 frames per second.

Low-end bass frequencies (ranging between 20 Hz and 150 Hz) are continuously sampled to detect transient spikes. When an impulse exceeds the moving threshold, the character's hips, knees, and torso dynamically flex into rhythmically accurate dance choreography.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does uploading a song consume mobile data or send my file to a server?

No. Your music file never leaves your device. All decoding, frequency calculations, and canvas animations are processed directly inside your browser's local JavaScript engine.

Why did the dancer stop when I switched browser tabs?

Mobile and desktop browsers throttle requestAnimationFrame in background tabs to preserve battery life and memory. Switching back to the tab restores animation immediately.

Can I dance to songs with no heavy drum beats?

Yes, though dance steps react most noticeably to clear percussive kicks and bass drops. For melodic or vocal-centric tracks, decrease the Beat Sensitivity slider to register subtle mid-tone transients.
