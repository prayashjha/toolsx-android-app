🎚️ Audio Quality Guide: 128 vs 192 vs 320 kbps

  • 128 kbps — Standard QualityBest for podcasts, voiceovers, and spoken content. Smallest file size, mobile-friendly streaming. 128 kbps is perfectly sufficient for human voice.
  • 192 kbps — High QualityIdeal for music tracks, YouTube background scores, and casual listening. Balanced file size and quality — the most popular choice among creators.
  • 320 kbps — Studio QualityFor audiophiles, professional musicians, and high-end headphone users. Highest fidelity but also largest file size. Use when quality cannot be compromised.

🔊 Pro Tips for Smooth Conversion

  • Prefer smaller filesVideos longer than 30 minutes can strain browser memory. If possible, trim the video first with a video editor.
  • Use Chrome or EdgeThese browsers offer the best compatibility with Web Audio API and the LAME encoder. Firefox works too but may be slower.
  • Close background appsDuring conversion, close heavy applications (video editing, gaming) so the CPU can focus and conversion completes faster.
  • Verify with headphonesAfter download, listen to the MP3 with good headphones to verify quality, especially if you used 128 kbps.
