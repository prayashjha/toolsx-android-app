❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    
Is Analog Tape Deck Studio free?

    
Yes, it is 100% free with no signup or hidden charges. All processing happens in your browser.

    
How does the AI prompt generator work?

    
It creates optimized prompts for Suno and Udio. You select genre, mood, BPM, key, vocal type, and textures, then copy the generated prompt.

    
What is the Camelot Key system?

    
The Camelot system helps DJs and producers with harmonic mixing. Each musical key has a code like 8A (A Minor). Compatible keys can be mixed smoothly.

    
How do I use the BPM Delay Calculator?

    
Enter your song BPM and the tool calculates quarter, eighth, sixteenth, and dotted delay times in milliseconds for your DAW.

    
Can I use Hindi or Hinglish lyrics?

    
Yes! English, Hindi, Hinglish, and Maithili are supported. Select the language and the AI prompt will specify it.

    
What is the rhyme scheme guide?

    
It shows AABB (couplet) and ABAB (alternating) patterns. AABB works well for hooks and rap, while ABAB is common in ballads.

    
Why is syllable count important?

    
Syllable count ensures lyrics fit the rhythm. Each genre has a recommended range, like Hip-Hop 11-16 syllables per line.

    
Is my data private?

    
Absolutely. Everything runs client-side in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server, and no cookies are used.
