Prompt Writing Ka Secret

🎯 Perfect Prompt Formula:

  • Subject + Action: Kya hai aur kya kar raha hai (jaise "majestic lion standing on a cliff")
  • Setting + Background: Kahan hai, kaisa environment hai (jaise "African savanna at sunset")
  • Lighting: Golden hour, dramatic, soft, neon, cinematic
  • Style: Photorealistic, anime, watercolor, oil painting, 3D render
  • Quality modifiers: highly detailed, 8K, professional, sharp focus
