How to Use the Salary Slip Generator

  1. ✅ Choose whether you're an Employer generating this for someone, or an Employee generating your own payslip.
  2. ✅ Enter company name, address and, if you like, upload a logo — it stays only in your browser.
  3. ✅ Fill in employee details and type the Basic Salary — HRA, PF, Professional Tax and ESI auto-fill using common statutory rules.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Salary Slip Generator free to use?

Yes, it's completely free with no sign-up, and the downloaded PDF has no watermark.

How accurate are the auto-filled PF, HRA and Professional Tax amounts?

They follow common Indian payroll conventions — 40% of Basic for HRA, 12% employee PF (optionally capped at the ₹15,000 EPFO wage ceiling), and a typical Professional Tax slab. Professional Tax and PF policy can vary by state and employer, so review and edit any amount before finalising.

Is my salary data uploaded anywhere?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser, and saved company details stay only on your own device.

Can employees generate their own payslip, not just employers?

Yes — select "Employee — generating my own payslip" at the top, and the form relabels itself for that use case.

Why does only the payslip print, not the whole webpage?

Download / Print opens the payslip alone in a separate print view, so your printed or saved PDF never includes site navigation or other page content.
