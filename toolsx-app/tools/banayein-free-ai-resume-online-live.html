🎯 Ye Tool Kya Hai?

Tools-X ka ye advanced AI Resume Builder aur Portfolio Website Maker ek comprehensive solution hai jo professionals, students, aur job seekers ko minutes mein ek polished, recruiter-ready resume aur personal portfolio banane mein madad karta hai. Yeh tool 5 distinct professional templates offer karta hai — Modern (corporate clean look), Minimalist (elegant simplicity), Executive (senior leadership style), Creative (design-focused), aur Classic (traditional academic format). Har template ATS-friendly hai aur different industries ke liye optimized hai. Left side panel mein details bharte jaayein aur right side par turant live preview dekhein. AI Bullet Improver weak sentences ko strong, action-oriented phrases mein convert karta hai. Certifications aur Languages sections bhi included hain jo modern resumes ke liye essential hain.

🛠️ Kaise Use Karein

Resume creation bahut systematic hai. Pehle template choose karein — 5 options mein se jo aapki industry aur personality suit kare. Phir Personal Details section mein naam, title, contact info, aur professional summary bharein. Experience section mein "+ Add Job" button se multiple jobs add karein — har entry mein role, company, dates, aur achievements likhein. Education section mein degrees add karein. Skills mein comma-separated skills enter karein. Certifications mein AWS, Google, PMP jaise certifications add karein. Languages section mein language proficiency levels mention karein. Agar koi bullet weak lage (jaise "managed team"), to AI Bullet Improver mein type karke "Enhance" dabayein — turant professional version mil jayega jaise "Spearheaded cross-functional team driving 25% efficiency increase". Download PDF button se sanitized filename ke saath resume save karein.

✨ Advanced Features

Is tool ke standout features hain: (1) 5 Professional Templates — har ek unique design philosophy ke saath, Modern se Classic tak. (2) AI-Powered Bullet Enhancer — 15+ predefined patterns jo weak descriptions ko recruiter-friendly language mein convert karte hain. (3) Real-time Live Preview — har keystroke par instant update. (4) Certifications & Languages Sections — modern resume requirements. (5) PDF Export with Sanitized Filenames — security ke liye special characters automatically remove hote hain. (6) Portfolio Website Preview — ek complete personal website ka preview jo About, Experience, Projects, Skills, aur Contact sections ke saath ready hai. (7) 100% Browser-Based — koi server upload nahi, complete privacy. (8) Mobile Responsive — phone, tablet, desktop sab par perfectly kaam karta hai.

💡 Pro Tips

Best results ke liye: (1) Professional summary 2-3 lines mein rakhein jo aapki unique value proposition highlight kare. (2) Experience mein achievements quantify karein — "increased sales by 30%" better hai "improved sales" se. (3) Skills section mein relevant technologies prioritize karein. (4) Certifications section mein recency aur relevance matter karti hai — last 5 years ke certifications pehle rakhein. (5) Template selection industry ke hisaab se karein — Executive for C-suite, Creative for designers, Classic for academia. (6) AI Bullet Improver ka use karein par blindly copy na karein — apne actual achievements ke saath match karein. Yeh tool ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) ke liye optimized hai, lekin human readability bhi maintain karta hai.
