How to Use the Free GST Invoice Generator

Generating a professional, compliant tax invoice shouldn't require expensive software. The ToolsX generator allows freelancers, small business owners, and agencies to create standard invoices directly in the browser.

  1. Add Business Details: Upload your logo (optional) and enter your Company Name, GSTIN, and Address.
  2. Enter Client Info: Input the buyer's details. If they are registered for GST, include their GSTIN for B2B compliance. Ensure the Place of Supply is accurate.
  3. Toggle Tax Type: If your client is in a different state than your business, check the Apply IGST box. Otherwise, leave it unchecked for standard CGST/SGST splitting.
  4. Itemize Products/Services: Click Add New Item to list what you sold. Enter the HSN/SAC code, quantity, rate, and applicable GST percentage. The tool calculates line amounts automatically.
  5. Apply Discounts & Notes: Add a global discount percentage if applicable. Fill in your Bank Details and any specific Terms & Conditions for the footer.
  6. Print or Save as PDF: Click the Download PDF / Print button. Your browser's print dialog will open. Select Save as PDF as the destination to generate your file.

Privacy Note: 🔒 Your financial data never leaves your device. This tool relies on client-side JavaScript, meaning no data is uploaded to our servers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need an account to generate invoices?

No, this tool is completely free and requires no signup, login, or subscription. You can generate unlimited invoices instantly.

How does the discount feature work with GST?

According to standard accounting practices, the discount is applied to the subtotal first. GST is then calculated based on the reduced Taxable Value, ensuring you do not overcharge tax.

Can I generate an invoice without a GSTIN?

Yes. If you are an unregistered business or freelancer, simply leave the GSTIN fields blank. The tool will function as a standard billing generator, though you should enter 0 in the GST percentage fields if you are not authorized to collect tax.

Why did my logo not appear on the PDF?

Ensure you select a valid JPG or PNG file. Because the tool processes images locally in your browser for security, very large files might be blocked. Try a compressed logo under 1MB.
