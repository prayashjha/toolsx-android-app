How to Use the ToolsX Audio Editor

This free browser-based audio editor lets you trim, mix, and enhance music, podcasts, or voice recordings without installing any software.

📌 Step-by-Step Guide

1
Load your audio

Click "Upload Audio" or use "Record".

2
Select a region

Drag across the waveform to highlight the part.

3
Adjust and apply

Use Volume, Speed, Pitch, EQ, and Echo.

4
Export your file

Pick a file name and format, then export.

⚡ Tip: Undo (↶) keeps a full history of every edit.
🔒 Privacy: Runs entirely in your browser.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does this upload my files anywhere?

No. Everything happens locally.

Which formats can I upload?

MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and most browser-supported formats.

Can I undo mistakes?

Yes. Every edit is saved to undo history.
