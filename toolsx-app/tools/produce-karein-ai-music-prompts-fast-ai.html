❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    
Is SynthDeck Studio free?

    
Yes, it is 100% free. No signup or hidden charges. All processing happens in your browser, and your data never leaves your device.

    
How does the chord progression audio work?

    
Each chord plays real audio using the Web Audio API. The tones are generated procedurally, so they are copyright-free and require no samples. Triad notes play together for an authentic sound.

    
What is Groove Preview?

    
Groove Preview plays a 2-bar metronome click pattern at your selected BPM along with the tonic chord. It is real-time audio synthesis inside your browser.

    
What is the Camelot Key system?

    
The Camelot system helps DJs and producers with harmonic mixing. Each key gets a code like 8A (A Minor). Compatible keys can be mixed smoothly without clashing.

    
How do I save presets?

    
Click the Save button to store your current settings in localStorage. You can load or delete presets later from the preset chips.

    
Can I use the generated prompts in Suno or Udio?

    
Yes. The Prompt Engine creates AI-optimized prompts. Copy the text and paste it into Suno, Udio, or any similar music generator.

    
How does the BPM Delay Calculator work?

    
Enter your song BPM and the calculator automatically shows 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, and dotted delay times in milliseconds for your DAW.

    
Is my data private?

    
Absolutely. Everything runs client-side. No data is uploaded to any server, and presets are stored only in your browser's localStorage.
