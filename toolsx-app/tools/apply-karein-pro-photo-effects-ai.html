✅ How to Use AI Photo Effects Studio

Apply professional filters to your images in just a few steps:

  1. Upload your photo – click the drop area or drag & drop a PNG/JPEG file (max 5MB).
  2. Choose a filter – select from 12 options including B&W, Vintage, Noir, Sketch, and more.
  3. Adjust intensity – use the slider to control how strong the effect appears.
  4. Optional: rotate or flip – rotate 90° or flip horizontally if needed.
  5. Download the result – save your edited image as a lossless PNG file.

💡 Tip: For subtle vintage looks, set the intensity around 50-60%.
