✅ How to Use Movie Poster Studio

Create a professional poster for your film in a few simple steps:

  1. Enter your movie title – type the name of your film and customize title color.
  2. Choose a genre – Action, Romance, Horror, Sci-Fi, and more.
  3. Set the mood – Dark, Epic, Mystery, Colorful, or Futuristic.
  4. Pick the main character – Hero, Heroine, Couple, Team, Villain, etc.
  5. Select a style – Bollywood, Hollywood, Anime, Vintage, Modern, or others.
  6. Click Generate and download your HD PNG poster.

💡 Tip: Use short, ALL CAPS titles for the best typography.
