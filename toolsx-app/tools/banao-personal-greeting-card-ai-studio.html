✅ How to Use Festival Card Maker

Create a personalized greeting card in a few simple steps:

  1. Enter custom text – optionally type a name or message and customize text color.
  2. Choose a festival – select from 12 festivals including Diwali, Holi, Eid, and more.
  3. Pick a style – choose from 10 art styles like Traditional, Modern, Royal, or Watercolor.
  4. Select color palette – pick one of 8 color themes.
  5. Choose purpose – WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Personal, Office, or Story.
  6. Generate and download – click Generate Card, wait for AI, then download the HD PNG.

💡 Tip: Short custom texts work best. You can regenerate for a fresh variation anytime.
