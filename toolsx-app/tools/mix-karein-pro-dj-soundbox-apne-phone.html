🎯 How This Professional DJ Stage Works

Tools-X AI DJ Stage Studio v6 creates realistic DJ performance videos with advanced beat-reactive animation technology. 🔊 Beat-Reactive Speaker Cones

The highlight feature is physically moving speaker cones that push forward and backward based on bass intensity. Using Z-axis simulation, heavy bass creates up to 35% cone excursion with realistic shadow depth, surround ring expansion, and independent dust cap movement. The blue halo ring grows with cone excursion — bigger bass means bigger halo. This is the same cone excursion physics used in professional soundbox visualization software.

🎧 Realistic DJ Performance

The DJ performs realistic movements synchronized to the beat. One hand scratches the turntable with vinyl rotation tracking, the other tweaks mixer knobs with finger details, the head bobs with bass-reactive intensity, and the body sways to the rhythm. 💡 Professional Stage Lighting

The stage features 6 moving spotlights positioned around the venue. Each spotlight changes color on detected beats and tracks the DJ's position. Laser beams shoot across the stage on strong bass hits. Strobe lights flash on beat drops for dramatic effect. Smoke and particle effects create atmospheric haze. LED panel displays show live audio waveforms. All lighting is calculated in real-time based on audio frequency analysis.

🥁 Beat Detection Technology

The beat-detection engine uses Web Audio API to analyze 256 frequency bins in real-time. Bass frequencies (first 12 bins) are isolated and monitored for spikes above the moving average. When a beat is detected, speaker cones push forward, spotlights change colors, laser beams fire, and the DJ transitions to new movements. The system maintains fast response times with 0.5 smoothing for immediate visual feedback.
