❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this ringtone maker free?

Yes, it is 100% free. No hidden charges, no signup, no watermark. You can generate and download unlimited ringtones.

Is the downloaded ringtone copyright-free?

Absolutely. All music is generated procedurally using the Web Audio API – no pre-recorded samples are used.

How does the voice announcement work?

The browser's built-in Speech Synthesis API plays the voice in the preview. The downloaded WAV contains only instrumental music; you can add the voice later with an external editor.

How do I set the WAV file as a ringtone?

Android: Settings > Sound > Phone Ringtone > Add ringtone. iPhone: Convert to .m4r with iTunes or GarageBand, then sync.

How many music styles are available?

There are 15 styles: Romantic, Soft Flute, Bollywood Groove, Bhakti, EDM, Chill Lofi, Smooth Sax, Acoustic Guitar, Desi Punjabi, Marimba Bell, Nature Calm, Classical Piano, Cinematic Epic, Retro 90s, and Corporate Minimal.

Is Hindi voice supported?

Yes! Hindi (Indian) and Indian English voices are available. Best results in Chrome and Edge.

How do I adjust the music and voice volume?

Use the Music vs Voice slider. Left side increases music, right side increases voice. Default is 55% balanced.

Is my data safe?

Completely. All processing happens client-side in your browser. No data is uploaded, tracked, or stored externally.
